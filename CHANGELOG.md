## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-30

### Added

- Time scale: real time axis with adaptive buckets from weeks to minutes, drag to select, drag again to zoom, presets, deploy and version-change markers.
- Commit timeline on a single spine with date and time, gap-to-previous labels, group chips, and "Live on" markers.
- Stat tiles, change footprint, versions ladder (compact card and full drawer), activity calendar, deploy drift, who-touches-what heatmap with a Leader row, authors.
- Commit drawer with changed files, color-coded diff, and Deploy, Revert, and Commit-pending actions, each behind a confirmation.
- Worker Groups and Edge Fleets sectioned in every list, legend, filter, and chart.
- Light and dark theme from the Cribl shell; VisiCore accent on focal elements.
- Marketplace packaging: policies declared in `config/policies.yml`, CI and tag-triggered release workflows.
